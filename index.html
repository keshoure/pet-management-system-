Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PawPulse features, passports, and records.

Is my pet's medical data stored securely?
Yes! All pet profiles, weight curves, and medical entries are stored with local persistence in your browser. With our Plus and Unlimited tiers, data is securely encrypted in transit and at rest.
Can I export or print my pet's digital passport?
Absolutely. In the Passport tab, simply click "Export / Print" to generate a clean, official medical card with rabies certification, microchip ID, and emergency veterinary QR code ready for PDF export or paper printing.
Can I manage multiple dogs and cats in the same app?
Yes! PawPulse supports multi-pet management. You can switch between active pet profiles in a single click from the header or pets tab, with custom weight curves and vaccine schedules for each pet.
How does the vaccine countdown reminder work?
PawPulse automatically calculates the exact days remaining until your pet's next booster dose. Items due within 30 days display amber warning badges, while overdue items trigger notifications.
